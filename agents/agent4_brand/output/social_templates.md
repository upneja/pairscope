# Pairscope Social Sharing Copy

Templates for share cards generated from each report section. Each template includes copy for the share card image and the accompanying text post. Variables in `{{brackets}}` are populated dynamically from the user's report.

---

## General Sharing Guidelines

- Tone: Self-aware, curious, slightly self-deprecating. Never bragging, never alarming.
- The card should be intriguing without revealing the full insight. Drive clicks.
- Every card includes the Pairscope watermark and URL.
- Cards are designed to look good on Instagram Stories, Twitter/X, iMessage, and LinkedIn.

## Share Card Design Notes

- **Background:** Navy/slate, matching the report design
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif, white text
- **Layout:** Headline prominent, subtext smaller, Pairscope logo + URL at bottom
- **Dimensions:** 1080x1080 (Instagram), 1200x628 (Twitter/LinkedIn), 1080x1920 (Stories)
- **No photos of people** — abstract/geometric elements only
