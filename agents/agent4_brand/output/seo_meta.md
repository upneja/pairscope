## Additional Page-Level SEO Notes

- **H1:** Use the selected hero headline (recommend "See your relationship clearly.")
- **H2s:** "How It Works", "The Science", "Questions You're Already Asking" (FAQ)
- **Internal linking:** Link FAQ answers to future blog posts (e.g., "What is the Gottman Four Horsemen model?")
- **FAQ Schema:** Implement FAQ structured data using the landing page FAQ section to capture Google's FAQ rich snippets
- **Alt text for visuals:** Describe chart types and what they represent (e.g., "Radar chart showing Big Five personality traits relevant to relationship satisfaction")
