# Pairscope Launch Content Calendar

4-week plan (2 weeks pre-launch, 2 weeks post-launch). 3 posts per week across Twitter/X and LinkedIn. Optimized for the self-improvement, psychology, and tech audience.

**Posting cadence:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday

## Content Principles

1. **Lead with research, not product.** The science is the hook. Pairscope is the CTA.
2. **One insight per post.** Don't try to explain the whole product. Surface one compelling finding.
3. **Optimize for saves and shares, not likes.** The target audience bookmarks useful information.
4. **Twitter is for punchy threads.** LinkedIn is for professional framing of the same insights.
5. **Never be preachy.** Present findings with curiosity, not authority. "Here's what the research says" not "Here's what you should do."
6. **Always include the link.** Every post from Week 2 onward ends with pairscope.com.
