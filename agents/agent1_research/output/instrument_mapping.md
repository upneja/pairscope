## Sequencing Design Rationale

The `sequence_order` values follow these principles:

1. **Warm start (positions 1-5):** Begin with engaging scenario questions about conflict (Gottman Horsemen). These are concrete and relatable, reducing dropout.
2. **Deepen gradually (positions 6-18):** Move into Sound Relationship House dimensions and begin attachment items. Mix formats to prevent fatigue.
3. **Self-reflection block (positions 19-29):** Big Five personality items and deeper attachment questions. These require more introspection.
4. **Satisfaction check (positions 30-34, Mode A only):** CSI-4 items placed here as a natural "how's it going?" pause.
5. **Love Languages close (positions 33-44):** End with forced-choice love language pairs. These feel lighter and more fun, creating a positive finish.
6. **Format alternation enforced:** No more than 5 Likert items in a row anywhere in the sequence. Scenarios and forced-choice pairs are distributed to break up self-report fatigue.
